What MeowDrop does

Gentle remindersKind check-ins through your day, and quiet while you sleep.
A kitten to care forPet it, boop its nose, feed it treats and dress it up.
Your habits, explainedDaily, weekly and monthly views, patterns and a shareable monthly recap.
Friends & funOptional friends, a friendly leaderboard, streak rewards and the Kitty Pond game.

MeowDrop is a habit helper, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose anything. For health questions, please talk to a clinician.

Supporting MeowDrop

MeowDrop is free and has no ads. Inside the app, people who enjoy it can choose to send a small tip to its developer on a secure Razorpay page (UPI, card or wallet), by UPI QR code, or through Buy Me a Tea / Buy Me a Coffee.

  • Tips are completely voluntary. Every feature works without paying.
  • Tips go to the developer, Ritesh Naik, to keep the app free and growing. MeowDrop is not a registered charity, and tips are not charitable donations.
  • Nothing is sold in exchange for a tip: no goods, no subscriptions, no paid features.
  • Payments happen on Razorpay or in your own UPI app. MeowDrop never sees or stores your bank, card or UPI PIN details.
