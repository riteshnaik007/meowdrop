Delete your account

You can delete your MeowDrop account and its online data yourself, at any time, from inside the app. It takes under a minute.

How to delete it

  1. Open MeowDrop and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete my account.
  3. Read what will be removed, then tap Delete account.
  4. Google asks you to pick your account once more, to confirm it's really you.

You need an internet connection. If anything fails part-way, nothing is lost: your account stays as it was and you can simply try again.

What is deleted

Everything MeowDrop stores online for your account, straight away and permanently:

  • Your account record: name, email address and profile photo link.
  • Your public profile and friend code.
  • Your friends and friend requests.
  • What you shared with friends (today's progress, streak, kitten, Kitty Pond best, and litres if you switched that on).
  • Splashes, cheers and challenges you sent or received.
  • Your Google sign-in with MeowDrop.

MeowDrop keeps none of this after deletion. Google Firebase, which hosts it, may take a short time to clear it from its own systems.

What stays on your phone

Your water log, reminders, kitten and settings were never online, so they stay on your phone. To remove them too, use Settings > Your data and privacy > Delete all my data, or simply uninstall the app.

Never signed in?

Then there is no MeowDrop account and nothing is stored online. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
