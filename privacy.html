Privacy policy

MeowDrop is built to keep your water log on your phone. This page explains exactly what leaves it, and why.

Stays on your phone

  • Your water log, daily goal, reminder schedule and routine.
  • The profile details you enter to estimate a goal (such as age, height and weight).
  • Your kitten, rewards, quiz answers and game scores.

MeowDrop has no analytics trackers. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one, and now and then between Kitty Pond games (see "Ads" below). It does not use your location, contacts or microphone. The motion sensor is used only for the tilt and shake effects while the app is open, and nothing from it is stored.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in is only needed for friends features. We use Google Firebase to sign you in and keep a small account record: your name, email address and profile photo link. It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Mumbai (asia-south1).

If you share with friends (optional, off until you turn it on)

Only friends you accept can see:

  • How far through today's goal you are (a percentage), whether the goal is done and when you last sipped.
  • Your goal streak, goal days this week, your kitten's name and look, and your best Kitty Pond score.
  • Today's litres and goal, only if you separately switch on "Show my litres".

Friends never see your history, profile details or email. Switching sharing off removes this from their screens. Splashes, cheers and challenges you send carry your display name and nothing you type.

Ads

MeowDrop shows ads from Google AdMob in two places only: when you choose to watch an ad to give the kitten a treat, and occasionally between Kitty Pond games. Ads never appear in reminders, while you log water, during setup, in the widget, or right after you reach your goal.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how ads are viewed and interacted with, under Google's own policies. Your water log, profile and reminders are never shared with AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Tips and payments

In the MeowDrop app from Google Play, tips are paid through Google Play billing. Google Play handles the payment under Google's own privacy policy. The app only learns which treat was bought, so it can say thank you and count your treats on your phone; it never receives your payment details, and this information is not sent to MeowDrop's servers.

On this website, tips are paid on Razorpay's secure payment page, in your own UPI app, or on the Buy Me a Tea / Buy Me a Coffee websites. Razorpay handles those payments under its own privacy policy; MeowDrop never receives or stores your payment details.

Your choices

  • Use MeowDrop without signing in; everything except friends works.
  • Export or delete your data from Settings > Your data and privacy.
  • Delete your account and all its online data yourself in the app: Settings > Account > Delete my account. How account deletion works.
